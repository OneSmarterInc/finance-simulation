# How the course works

Your team runs a manufacturing firm that makes two products and sells them in three regions. The firm has already run for three quarters before you take over, so you begin with three full sets of statements to read. This course is about reading and explaining those statements. The operating decisions you make each quarter are kept simple on purpose: their job is to give your firm a new set of results to interpret.

## Each quarter

When a quarter opens, your team enters its decisions on the Decisions page. Every field starts from what your firm did last quarter, so you can change only what you want to change. Anyone on the team can save a draft or submit, and the decisions belong to the team. You can keep editing until the deadline. At the deadline the quarter closes, your results are calculated and the next quarter opens. If your team has not submitted by then, your previous quarter's decisions are used again and your history shows that nothing was submitted.

## Reading your results

Your dashboard shows where the firm stands now, and History shows every quarter side by side, with the decisions that produced each one. Read the four reports together: the income statement says what the firm earned, the balance sheet what it owns and owes, the cash flow statement where the cash went, and the notes how the numbers were measured. A firm can report a profit while its cash falls, and a choice of accounting method can change reported income without changing a dollar of cash. Your instructor will ask you to explain what changed and why.

## Accounting methods

If your instructor allows it, your team can ask to change how the firm reports, for example its depreciation or inventory method. One request per team per quarter, with a written reason. Your instructor approves or declines it. A change approved before the quarter closes applies to that quarter's results, otherwise from the next quarter, and that quarter's notes disclose the change and your reason.
