## The evaluation stage

In this course the last quarters add an evaluation stage. Every firm's published statements and notes become visible to the other teams, and for each rival your team receives a consultant's valuation built from what that firm reported. Your team builds its own view of what a controlling stake is worth, places a sealed offer it can afford to fund, and defends its price. When your instructor settles the stage, each firm's true worth is shown beside the price paid.
