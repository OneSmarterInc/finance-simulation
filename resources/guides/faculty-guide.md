# Running your course

This guide walks through a course from setup to the last quarter. The course teaches accounting interpretation. The operating decisions (how much to make, what to charge, where to buy) are there to give every team a fresh and different set of statements each quarter, so keep the class's attention on reading and explaining those statements rather than on the operating side.

## Setting up

The course wizard takes you through six steps: the course name and dates, the quarter schedule with a decision deadline for each quarter, which decision areas open in which quarter, the accounting methods every firm starts with, co-faculty, and deployment. Quarter 4 is the first quarter students control, and it must open forecasting and sourcing. Until you open production, the plant makes what each team forecasts, less the units it already has in stock; once you open it, teams set the units to make themselves. An area stays open once you open it. The starting methods are the ones each firm reports with in its warm-up and keeps until a change is approved; two sections of the same course can start from different methods, which makes a good class comparison. Inventory is always first-in, first-out. If you want students to choose their own accounting methods, turn on student accounting-election control; each team can then ask to change a method once per quarter, with a written reason, and you approve or decline. If you add the evaluation stage, the course becomes Flexee Financial Accounting+ and needs enough quarters for the extra stage at the end.

## Where demand comes from

Customers decide how many units each firm sells. Every firm's market grows from the size it reached in the warm-up, and from Quarter 4 every firm's market grows and swings with the same draws, so the gaps between firms come from their decisions. Demand is seasonal: Quarters 4 and 8 are the busiest and Quarters 5 and 9 the quietest. Price and the marketing budget change how much customers order. A team's forecast is its estimate of those orders, so a wrong forecast shows up in the statements: units nobody ordered stay in stock and are sold first next quarter, and orders the team couldn't fill are lost. The revenue note shows units ordered, units sold and orders not filled, and the inventory note shows the stock carried into the next quarter. Two operating choices also reach the accounting. A supplier's lead time sets how much is still on the way at quarter end, and under FOB shipping point those goods sit in the firm's inventory with the bill in accounts payable. Defects that get past inspection come back as returns or warranty claims, so a team that skips inspection sees its claims run past its warranty estimate. Inventory is always first-in, first-out. Courses created before October 2026 keep the earlier engine, where demand followed each team's forecast.

## Firms and students

Students join from the course link on the Students page. Create the firms before Quarter 4 opens and place each registered student in a firm, either on the Firms page or directly from the Students page. Each firm's three warm-up quarters are generated when it is created, so every team starts with a history to read. Firms cannot be added once Quarter 4 has opened, because a new firm would have no Quarter 4 to build on, and students cannot move between firms once their firm has history.

## Quarters run themselves

Quarter 4 opens on its start date. Each quarter then stays open until its deadline, even if every firm has submitted early, and at the deadline the system locks the decisions, calculates every firm's results and opens the next quarter. A firm that has not submitted by the deadline keeps its previous quarter's decisions, and its history says so. You can extend the open quarter's deadline from the Quarters page, and if a firm's results fail to calculate you can retry that firm there; the next quarter waits until every firm has closed.

## Reading results with the class

Quarter results show one report at a time: a summary of the trend, the decisions behind each quarter, the income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement and notes. You can read one firm across all its quarters or compare every firm in a single quarter side by side. The decisions tab says who made each choice: the team, the simulation for areas you have not opened yet, or the warm-up.

## Accounting-method requests

When a team asks to change a method, the request shows the firm's current methods beside the proposed ones, the team's reason and the quarter the change would take effect. If you approve while the quarter it was filed in is still open, the change applies to that quarter's results; otherwise it applies from the next quarter. The statements for that quarter carry a note disclosing the change and the team's reason, as a change in accounting principle requires. If you decline, write a short note; the team sees it.

## The evaluation stage

On a Flexee Financial Accounting+ course, the evaluation stage opens automatically with its first quarter. At that moment every firm's membership freezes and every firm's published statements become visible to its rivals, and this cannot be undone, so the Quarters page and your dashboard remind you as the date approaches. During the stage you can see how many offers each firm has received, never the amounts. Once the last quarter has closed you press Settle, and students then see the reveal, which lets you run it live in class.
